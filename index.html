How to read this list

Light is the range each plant will tolerate, not a preference. A plant marked “full sun” and “part shade” will grow in both; it simply blooms hardest in the sun. Use the filter to see everything that will take a given exposure.

Companions are drawn from Sparrowhawk’s own catalogue by shared light and moisture, with a nudge toward a different canopy layer and toward plants already on this list. Where the nursery prints a companion note, it appears in the plant’s panel.

Stock reflects the quantities you recorded. Eleven species are marked unavailable — the profiles are still here so the list is complete when the spring sale opens.
