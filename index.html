How to read this list

Light is the range each plant will tolerate, not a preference. A plant marked “full sun” and “part shade” will grow in both; it simply blooms hardest in the sun. Use the filter to see everything that will take a given exposure.

Companions are habitat-fit suggestions drawn from Sparrowhawk’s catalogue. They combine shared light and moisture with Pacific Northwest ecosystem affinities, canopy layering, and plants already on this list. They are regional matches, not strict planting prescriptions.
